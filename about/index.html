FAQ

What is the relationship between taxonomy and phylogeny?

Taxonomy is for naming and grouping organisms, while phylogeny describes evolutionary history. While taxonomy aims to mirror phylogeny, they can differ in some cases due to evolving understanding or legacy categorizations. We use NCBI Taxonomy, which is a classification system and may not reflect every recent phylogenetic discovery.

Where did the idea for PhyloGuessr come from?

I once attended a 'Science on Tap' trivia night, and they played a game like this on powerpoint slides...I had always kept a list of fun facts from that event and thought it would be fun to make it into a web-based game. It is also inspired by GeoGuessr.

Can I use this for my own trivia night or classroom?

Yes! Feel free to use it for any purpose. You can even change it for your needs. It is free and open source
